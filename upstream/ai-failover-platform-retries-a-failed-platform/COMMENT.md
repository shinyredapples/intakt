A note for the `FailoverProvider`, so it does not carry over a bug of `FailoverPlatform` (BETA1, still on `main` 17d320e7). `do()` puts a failed platform into `$failedPlatforms` and consumes a token of its limiter, but the next call tries every platform in order again: the limiter only decides when a platform leaves `$failedPlatforms`, never whether it is skipped, so a platform that is down is called first on every call. The limiter key is `$platform::class`, and the OpenAi, Ollama and Anthropic factories all return `Platform`, so every platform shares one limiter (the documented Ollama-to-OpenAI example included), and successful calls consume a token of it too. Expected: a platform in `$failedPlatforms` is skipped until its own limiter allows a retry.
